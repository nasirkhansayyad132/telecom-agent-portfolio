## Data add-ons and fair usage

- Add-ons stack on top of base plans and expire in 30 days or when consumed.
- Popular options: 5GB for AFN 250, 15GB for AFN 600, Night Owl 20GB (11pm-6am) for AFN 350.
- Activate add-ons via the AWCC app or USSD *555# (Add-ons menu); confirmations arrive by SMS.
- Fair usage triggers after 300GB on unlimited plans, throttling to 1 Mbps until next cycle.
- Usage can be checked via the mobile app or by dialing *888#.
- Unused add-on data does not roll over.
