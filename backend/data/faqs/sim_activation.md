## SIM activation steps

- New SIMs activate within 2 minutes after first network attach.
- If not active, dial *123# and choose "Activate SIM".
- KYC check: national ID must match registration record; mismatches block activation until corrected.
- eSIM QR codes can be reused up to 3 times; delete old profiles before retrying.
- Activation failures over 30 minutes are eligible for a free 2GB apology pack.
