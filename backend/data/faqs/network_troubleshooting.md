## Poor mobile data speeds

- Restart the phone and toggle airplane mode for 20 seconds to refresh the session.
- Confirm signal is at least 2 bars. If indoors, move near a window.
- Check APN: `telecom-net` with default proxy/port.
- If speeds stay under 1 Mbps for 30 minutes, collect speed test, location, and device model for escalation.
- Outage updates post to status.telecom.local; we waive overage charges during confirmed outages.
