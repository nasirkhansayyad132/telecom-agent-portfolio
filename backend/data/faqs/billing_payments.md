## How to pay your bill

- Supported channels: mobile app, *555# menu, bank cards (Visa/Mastercard), and cash at partner stores.
- Late fees: AFN 50 applied after day 5; services suspend on day 15 until payment posts.
- Receipts are emailed within 5 minutes; SMS confirmation arrives within 15 minutes.
- If payment is duplicated, a refund is auto-initiated within 24 hours.
