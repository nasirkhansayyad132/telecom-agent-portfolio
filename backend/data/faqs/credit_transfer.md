# Credit Transfer (e-Top Up share)

You can easily transfer balance (credit) to another AWCC subscriber using the Credit Transfer service.

## How to Transfer Credit

**Via USSD Code:**
Dial `*444*<RecipientNumber>*<Amount>#` and press call.
Example: `*444*0701234567*50#` to send 50 AFN.

**Via SMS:**
Send an SMS to 444 with the format: `TR <RecipientNumber> <Amount>`

## Rules and Fees
- Minimum transfer amount: 10 AFN
- Maximum transfer amount: 500 AFN per transaction
- Service fee: 2 AFN per transaction
- You must have sufficient balance remaining after the transfer.
