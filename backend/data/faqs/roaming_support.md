## International roaming checklist

- Enable data roaming and restart the device after landing.
- Preferred partner networks: GulfTel, EuroConnect, and AsiaLink; manual network selection may improve service.
- Standard roaming rates apply unless a roaming pack is purchased before travel.
- VoLTE may be unavailable on partner networks; fallback to 3G/2G is expected.
- If roaming is blocked, verify bill is paid and SIM age is >48 hours.
